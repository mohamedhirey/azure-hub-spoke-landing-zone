## 5. The whole subscription, not only this deployment's resource groups

Two things outlive the four steps above, and neither belongs to this deployment. Azure creates a
`NetworkWatcherRG` resource group by itself the first time a virtual network appears in a region,
and `az ad app delete` only moves the app registration to the directory's recycle bin.
